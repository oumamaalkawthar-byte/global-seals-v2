PRODUCT QUESTIONS

Useful to know.

Can a plastic seal be reused?+

Security seals are intended to show interference. Ask for a model suited to your closure and replace it after authorised opening.

Can I add company identification?+

Ask about available marking methods, colours and serial numbering for the selected model.
