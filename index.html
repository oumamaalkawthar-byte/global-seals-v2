BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

A clear starting point.

Practical answers for your next procurement enquiry.

What should I include in a quote enquiry?

Share the application, product if known, approximate quantity, marking requirements and timeline. Include locking-point dimensions when relevant.

How do I choose between the six product types?

Start with what you are sealing and how the closure will be inspected. Compare the product range, then ask our team to confirm a suitable model and specification.

Can I request numbering or custom marking?

Tell us your preferred numbering, logo or barcode requirements. Options depend on the exact supplied model; confirm availability with our team.

How should a seal be checked at handover?

Check its presence, compare visible identification with shipment records and inspect its condition before authorised removal. Follow your organisation's procedure if anything is unexpected.

Does this form place an order?

No. It prepares an email enquiry in your email app. Send the draft to start a discussion about suitability, availability and a quotation.
