Electric meters, valves & cabinets

Choose a wire closure based on the sealing holes, available clearance and your inspection procedure.

  • Electric meter covers

    Measure the cover sealing point and check the meter operator’s specification before selecting a single-use closure. Meter Seal

  • Valves and access cabinets

    For paired sealing points that accept a rotary closure. Roto Seal

  • Crimped inspection seals

    For procedures that call for a stamped impression. Lead Seal

  • Replacement wire

    Match the diameter and length to your lead or roto seal. Sealing Wire
