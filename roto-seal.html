PRODUCT QUESTIONS

Useful to know.

How is a roto seal applied?+

Pass compatible wire through the sealing points and the seal body, then rotate the handle to retain it. Follow the supplied model’s instructions.

Can I request a barcode?+

Barcode and logo options are listed in the catalogue. Confirm space, format and availability for your order.
