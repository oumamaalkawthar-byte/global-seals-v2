Inspecting a Seal at Destination: A Simple Checklist

Use seal inspection as part of your established receiving procedure before authorised removal.

Check presence

Confirm that a seal is present at the intended locking point. Do not treat a seal alone as proof that the contents are unchanged.

Compare identification

Read the visible number and compare it with the shipment records. Record a missing or mismatched identifier rather than assuming it is correct.

Inspect condition

Look for visible damage or signs of interference around the seal and locking point. Follow your organisation's escalation procedure if something does not match expectations.

Record before removal

Record the inspection result according to your receiving procedure. Only authorised personnel should remove the seal using the appropriate method for the supplied model.

Product reference: Global Seals product information. Confirm the exact supplied model and your operating requirements with the team.
