SECURITY SEAL APPLICATION NOTES

Meter Seals: Where They Fit in Routine Utility Checks

A suitable seal gives a clear reference when checking access to meter enclosures and related equipment.

Identify the sealing points

Describe the enclosure and the points the seal or wire must pass through. Share dimensions when requesting product guidance.

Discuss the locking format

Meter, roto and other wire seals use different closure arrangements. Ask which supplied model suits your application and access requirements.

Plan routine checks

Include seal presence, identification and visible condition in your established inspection procedure. A seal supports these checks rather than replacing them.

Check compatibility before ordering

For wire-based closures, confirm the compatible wire specification and length. Ask about marking options for the exact model.

Product reference: Global Seals product information. Confirm the exact supplied model and your operating requirements with the team.
