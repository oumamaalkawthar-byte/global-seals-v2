SECURITY SEAL APPLICATION NOTES

Choosing the Right Container Seal for Your Shipment

Start with the closure, the identification you need and the checks made during each handover.

Define the application

Tell the supplier what you are sealing, how the locking point is arranged and the quantity required. A product name alone does not establish compatibility.

Confirm the identification

Discuss numbering and any marking requirements. Plan how the applied seal number will be recorded with the shipment documents and checked at each handover.

Confirm the supplied specification

Ask for the exact model, dimensions and removal method. If your operation requires a particular certification or transport standard, request supporting documentation for that model before ordering.

Prepare your enquiry

Include the application, approximate quantity, preferred marking and required timeline. Global Seals can help you discuss an appropriate sealing solution.

Product reference: Global Seals product information. Confirm the exact supplied model and your operating requirements with the team.
